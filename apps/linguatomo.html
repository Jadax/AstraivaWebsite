About LinguaTomo

An offline-first Japanese companion from your first kana to advanced study. 1,800+ words across CEFR & JLPT tiers, grammar, handwriting practice, spaced repetition and seasonal stories. No ads, no paywalls, and your progress never leaves your device.

Features

  • 1,800+ words organised across CEFR & JLPT tiers
  • Grammar, handwriting and reading practice in one place
  • Spaced repetition that adapts to what you actually forget
  • Seasonal stories starring the Leo mascot
  • Offline-first — works without an internet connection
