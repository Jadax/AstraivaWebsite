About Stumped!

Run the club from the boardroom to the final over, transfers, training, tactics, finances and the media circus. Every selection, auction bid and bowl change has a consequence, and the match engine turns each ball into a real decision.

Features

  • Full club management: transfers, training, tactics and finances
  • Ball-by-ball match engine with real decision points
  • Media and board pressure systems that test your nerve
  • Deep player development and statistics
  • Faithful, authentic cricket simulation for PC
Windows Android Management sim
