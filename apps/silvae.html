About Silvae

Silvae helps you keep houseplants, and outdoor ones, alive and thriving. Every plant gets a care plan that adapts to its pot, soil and the real weather at your location, not a generic "water weekly" guess. A free account keeps it all synced across your devices, with no ads and no paid tiers, ever.

Features

  • Personal care plans: watering and feeding schedules that adapt to pot size, soil and real weather
  • 400-species library with pet-safe filtering and care guides
  • Plant Doctor: identify a plant from a photo, or a quick symptom checklist for a likely cause and treatment
  • Growth journal: photos and notes over time, with before/after comparison
  • Gentle watering reminders, nothing more
