Privacy Policy

Your data stays yours.

Astraiva builds local-first, privacy-first software. We don't run servers that hold your data, we don't sell anything, and we don't track you. This page explains exactly what that means in practice.

Effective: July 31, 2026 Astraiva · Tushant Sharma

The short version

Astraiva products are designed to work on your device, without accounts and without the cloud. What you create — study progress, save games, squads, strategies — lives on your device and stays under your control. There is no telemetry, no advertising, and no profile being built about you.

In one sentence: we do not collect, sell, or share any personal data. Full stop.

Who we are

Astraiva is an independent, one-person studio founded by Tushant Sharma, the developer of Crown of Scars, Stumped!, LinguaTomo, VibeGaffer, ThetaForge, HorizonAI, Rovyniq, GPRO Strategy Tool, TM Advisor and FTP Advisor.

For any privacy question or request, email astraiva.apps@gmail.com. You'll reach a human — there's only one of us.

What we don't do

  • No accounts. None of our products require you to sign up or register.
  • No tracking. No analytics SDKs, no fingerprinting, no advertising identifiers.
  • No ads. We don't sell ads, and none of our products show third-party advertising.
  • No data selling or sharing. There is nothing to sell and no one we share with.
  • No AI training on your data. We don't have access to your data to train on.

What happens on your device

LinguaTomo & LinguaTomo Web

Your study progress, reviews and preferences are stored only on your device (in the app's local storage or in your browser's local storage on the web version). The app is offline-first: it works without an internet connection and nothing you study is transmitted to us.

Games — Crown of Scars, Stumped!

Save games and settings are written to local files on your machine. The games are offline single-player experiences with no online features and no connection to us.

Web tools — VibeGaffer, HorizonAI and others

These run entirely in your browser. Data you enter is processed locally and stored in your browser's storage. Nothing is sent to an Astraiva server — there isn't one.

Browser scripts — GPRO Strategy Tool, TM Advisor, FTP Advisor

These single-file Tampermonkey scripts run locally on the pages you install them on. They read the game pages you're viewing to give you advice and store any settings in your browser. They never transmit your data anywhere.

This website

  • No cookies are used for tracking, and there are no analytics scripts.
  • Your theme preference (dark/light) is remembered locally in your browser's storage.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address under Google's privacy policy.
  • Like any site, our hosting provider keeps standard server access logs (such as IP address and requested page) for security and reliability. We don't use them to identify you.

Third-party services

Where our products touch the outside world, they use established platforms rather than our own infrastructure. Each platform has its own privacy policy that governs that interaction:

  • App stores — Google Play, the App Store and Steam distribute our apps. Your store account and purchase details are handled by those platforms.
  • GitHub — hosts our open-source code and live web versions of some tools.
  • Ko-fi — if you support Astraiva, your payment is processed by Ko-fi/Stripe, not by us.

Children

Our games and apps are designed for a general audience and don't collect personal information from anyone — including children. Because nothing is collected, there is no age-gated data processing to speak of.

Your data, your control

Because your data lives on your device, you already have full control: clear the app's data, clear your browser storage, or uninstall — and everything is gone. You don't need to contact us to delete anything.

If you have any question about this policy or believe any statement here is wrong, please email astraiva.apps@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If we ever start collecting data, we will update this page first, with a new effective date, and make it obvious. This policy was last updated on July 31, 2026.
