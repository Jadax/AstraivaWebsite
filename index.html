Astraiva is an independent studio founded by Tushant Sharma that designs, builds and ships games, learning apps and practical tools, including Crown of Scars, Stumped!, LinguaTomo, VibeGaffer and more. Everything is built by hand, offline-first and privacy-first.

Astraiva was founded by Tushant Sharma, an independent developer and designer. He builds every product in the catalogue, from games in Godot 4 to Flutter apps like LinguaTomo to single-file browser tools.

Yes. LinguaTomo is completely free with no ads and no paywalls. It's an offline-first Japanese learning app covering 1,800+ words across CEFR & JLPT tiers, with grammar, handwriting practice and spaced repetition. You can try the live web version right from this site.

Crown of Scars is Astraiva's flagship game, a kingdom-building deck roguelike with 210 cards, five bosses and branching story events that remember how you fight. It is currently in development in Godot 4 for Windows.

Yes. Tools like VibeGaffer, TM Advisor and FTP Advisor are free and open source, run locally on your device, and collect no data. Astraiva runs no servers that hold your personal data and sells no ads. The website itself uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity for anonymous visitor statistics and session insights, only after you give consent.
