Privacy Policy

Crown of Scars keeps its secrets local.

Crown of Scars is an offline single-player deck roguelike. It doesn't collect, transmit or sell any personal data, your kingdom and your saves stay on your own machine.

Effective: August 1, 2026 Astraiva · Tushant Sharma

The short version

Crown of Scars is an offline single-player game. We don't collect any personal data. There are no analytics, no ads, no accounts and no online features. Your save games and settings stay on your device.

In one sentence: Crown of Scars is a local game with local saves, nothing about your play is sent anywhere.

What happens on your device

Save games, settings and preferences are stored locally on your machine (in the game's own save files). The game runs fully offline and never connects to a server or to Astraiva.

What we collect

  • No personal data. We don't ask for your name, email or any other information.
  • No usage data. There are no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in the game.
  • No advertising. Crown of Scars shows no third-party ads.
  • No device data. The game only reads and writes its own save files.

Third-party services

If Crown of Scars is distributed through a store like Steam or Google Play, that store handles the download and your store account under its own privacy policy. The game itself contains no third-party trackers or SDKs.

Children

Crown of Scars is designed for a general audience and doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because nothing is collected, there is no special processing for children's data.

Your data, your control

Since all data lives on your device, you already have full control: delete the game's save files or uninstall the game and everything is gone. You never need to contact us to delete anything.

Changes to this policy

If Crown of Scars ever gains online features or collects data, this page will be updated first, with a new effective date, before the game changes. Last updated on August 1, 2026.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email astraiva.apps@gmail.com. You'll reach the developer directly.
