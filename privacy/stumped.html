Privacy Policy

Stumped! keeps your game on your device.

Stumped! is a fully offline cricket management simulation. It doesn't collect, transmit or sell any personal data — your club lives entirely on your own machine.

Effective: August 1, 2026 Astraiva · Tushant Sharma

The short version

Stumped! is an offline single-player game. We don't collect any personal data. There are no analytics, no ads, no accounts and no network features. Your save games and settings stay on your device.

In one sentence: Stumped! is a local game with local saves — nothing about your play is sent anywhere.

What happens on your device

Save games, settings and preferences are stored locally on your machine (in the game's own save files). The game works fully offline and never connects to a server or to Astraiva.

What we collect

  • No personal data. We don't ask for your name, email or any other information.
  • No usage data. There are no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in the game.
  • No advertising. Stumped! shows no third-party ads.
  • No location, contacts or media. The game never touches your device's other data.

Third-party services

If you install Stumped! through a store like Google Play, that store handles the download and your store account under its own privacy policy. The game itself contains no third-party trackers or SDKs.

Children

Stumped! is designed for a general audience and doesn't collect personal information from anyone — including children. Because nothing is collected, there is no special processing for children's data.

Your data, your control

Since all data lives on your device, you already have full control: delete the game's save files or uninstall the game and everything is gone. You never need to contact us to delete anything.

Changes to this policy

If Stumped! ever gains online features or collects data, this page will be updated first, with a new effective date, before the app changes. Last updated on August 1, 2026.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email astraiva.apps@gmail.com. You'll reach the developer directly.
