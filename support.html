Common questions

  • Is anything behind a paywall? No. Everything is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
  • Why do you ask for analytics consent? We use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity for anonymous visitor stats, only after you accept. See the privacy policy.
  • Do the browser tools collect my data? No. Tools like VibeGaffer, TM Advisor and FTP Advisor run entirely in your browser and send nothing anywhere.
  • When will Crown of Scars or Stumped! release? When they're ready — no dates promised, because quality comes first. Follow the studio to be first to know.

Still stuck?

Email astraiva.apps@gmail.com and you'll reach the founder directly. Include what you're using, what you expected and what happened instead — it makes a reply much faster.

In one sentence: open-source tools get help fastest via GitHub issues; everything else is one email away.
